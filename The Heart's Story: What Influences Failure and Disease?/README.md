# Visualizing Heart Disease and Heart Failure Risk Factors

## Overview

This project utilizes Tableau to explore and visualize the key risk factors associated with heart disease and heart failure. The data story is designed to be accessible and informative, particularly for a general audience, to promote understanding and encourage proactive heart health. An interactive risk calculator is included, allowing users to estimate their personal risk probabilities.

## Key Features

* **Data Story:** A multi-frame Tableau story that guides users through the complex relationships between various risk factors and heart conditions.
* **Target Audience:** Primarily designed for a general audience (e.g., Akari Yamaguchi), with a focus on clear and actionable information.
* **Key Risk Factors:** Explores factors such as age, BMI, blood pressure, cholesterol levels, smoking status, diabetes, family history, and kidney disease.
* **Interactive Risk Calculator:** A dedicated page where users can input their personal health data to receive estimated probabilities of developing heart failure, heart disease, and kidney disease.
* **Simple and Intuitive Visualizations:** Employs straightforward chart types (bar charts, scatter plots, highlight tables) to maximize clarity and ease of understanding.
* **Clear Visual Hierarchy:** Uses color and font size strategically to guide the user's attention and highlight key information.

## Design Choices

The following design choices were made with the primary audience (general public) in mind:

1.  **Use of Simple and Intuitive Chart Types:**
    * For users with limited medical knowledge, clarity is paramount. The project avoids complex visualizations in favor of bar charts, scatter plots, and highlight tables.
    * This ensures that the information is easily digestible and that key insights are readily apparent.

2.  **Emphasis on Color and Clear Visual Hierarchy:**
    * Consistent color coding is used to represent the presence or absence of heart conditions.
    * Larger fonts and strategic use of white space help to draw attention to the most important information.
    * The story follows a logical flow, making it easy for users to follow the narrative.

3.  **Interactive Personalized Risk Calculator:**
    * This feature allows users to input their own health data and receive personalized risk assessments.
    * It directly addresses the audience's need for information about their own potential health risks.
    * The calculator is designed to be user-friendly and accessible to individuals with limited technical or medical knowledge.

## Project Structure

The Tableau Public workbook is organized as a data story with the following key components:

* **Overview of Risk Factors:** Visualizations showing the prevalence of various risk factors and their relationship to heart disease and heart failure.
* **Interactive Risk Calculator:** A page where users can input their data.
